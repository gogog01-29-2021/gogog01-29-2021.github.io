### July 7 — Main Conference (Day 1)

**Invited Talk — Causal Inference with Transformer Models**
Susan Athey (Stanford GSB) · Hall C · 4:00–5:00 PM KST

> Answering causal questions about *sequence* data — text, career/job sequences,
> customer journeys. Methods for average treatment effects, conditional ATEs, and
> decompositions of across-group differences in average outcomes, under both
> experimental and observational data.

Speaker: Susan Athey — The Economics of Technology Professor, Stanford GSB;
2023 President of the American Economic Association; Chief Economist, U.S. DOJ
Antitrust Division (2022–2024); founding director, Golub Capital Social Impact Lab.
